Company

Two decades of relevant industry experience

Leeward Support Services W.L.L is a Bahrain-based provider of manpower, facilities management, contracting, and technical support services. The company was established by experienced professionals with over 20 years of collective hands-on experience in US Navy support services and military installation operations.

That experience covers operational management, service delivery, and the standards expected in demanding military environments, including US and UK naval-base settings and DODDS-related operations. It includes military procedures, access control, confidentiality, personnel vetting, safety, and compliance.

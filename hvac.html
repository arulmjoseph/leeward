The service

What this covers

Climate control is operational infrastructure. Leeward covers central air conditioning, VRF/VRV, chillers, and cold storage, from planned maintenance and energy retrofits through emergency call-out when a system cannot wait.

  • Central air conditioning, VRF/VRV, chillers, and cold storage

Questions about HVAC

Which systems are covered?+

Central air conditioning, VRF/VRV, chillers, and cold storage.

What kind of work is included?+

Design, installation, maintenance, and emergency repair for those climate-control systems.

Is emergency support available?+

Yes. 24/7 emergency HVAC support is part of the service for breakdowns that cannot wait for a planned visit.

Can the plant be reviewed, not only repaired?+

Energy audits and retrofits are included, so a system can be checked against the building as well as repaired when it fails.
